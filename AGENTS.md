# Pep Rally product guide

Pep Rally is a creator-led marketplace for practical mini-apps built from real-life solutions. Preserve the warm, energetic, human feel: cream backgrounds, coral/yellow/pink/green accents, editorial serif headlines, strong navy outlines, playful badges, and concise conversational copy.

## Product principles

- Start with the user outcome, decision, or job to be done—not the technology.
- Keep the experience welcoming to nontechnical people. Use plain language and progressive disclosure.
- Ask only the minimum context needed to produce a useful result. Prefer a few meaningful questions over a long generic intake.
- Make the creator visible. Explain why they built the tool, who it is for, and what real experience shaped it.
- Make trust legible. State how a tool was tested, what assumptions it makes, what inputs it uses, and what the buyer should review themselves.
- Keep deterministic calculations in code. Do not present generated or calculated claims without checking their inputs and math.
- Preserve human judgment. High-stakes outputs must include an appropriate review step and should never imply guaranteed accuracy.
- Build the smallest end-to-end version first. Avoid arbitrary code uploads, complex creator payouts, and speculative platform features until demand is validated.
- For durable changes, verify the production build before publishing. Keep private data, credentials, and secrets out of the repository.

## Current MVP

The primary proof is a buyer discovering a creator-led tool, understanding its value and provenance, entering a few inputs, previewing a personalized result, and choosing to add it to their rally. The bachelorette planner is the flagship demo. Marketplace search, purchase state, and creator submission are prototypes rather than production commerce or persistence.

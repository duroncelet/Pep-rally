# Pep Rally demo brief

## The one-line product

Pep Rally is a marketplace where people can try, buy, and sell small working apps that turn real information into a finished, portable outcome.

## The five-minute demo

1. **Start at the storefront.** Say: “This is not a template marketplace. Every Rally is a working mini-app with a specific finish line.”
2. **Open the free Bachelorette Blueprint.** Add the bride, destination, budget comfort, guest needs, a stay, and an itinerary. Show the money ledger and ready-to-send group update.
3. **Download the outcome.** The buyer keeps a clean Markdown plan with the brief, budget, lodging shortlist, itinerary, reservations, tasks, and packing list.
4. **Open the free Little Garden Planner.** Change the space, sun, crops, and location. Load live weather, show the proposed layout, and download the garden plan.
5. **Return to the marketplace.** Open a paid Rally preview, create a first result, and show that its outcome can be saved or downloaded. Then open “Build or publish a Rally” to show the creator side.

## The customer outcome

The product is the usable mini-app. The app helps the buyer finish a job and keep the result in a private workspace. Markdown, calendar, CSV, PDF, links, messages, or booking handoffs are portable outputs—not substitutes for the app.

## What is working in this private prototype

- A buyer-first marketplace and full listings
- Two free, usable case-study Rallies
- Saved private Bachelorette and garden workspaces
- Live Open-Meteo weather data in the garden planner
- Downloadable Markdown outcomes
- Email, text, map, calendar, provider-link, and reservation handoffs where appropriate
- A creator studio for building or submitting a Rally
- Stripe checkout and payment-verification code, pending valid private test credentials

## What not to overclaim tonight

- Pep Rally does not yet host arbitrary uploaded creator code as an isolated production app.
- Creator payouts are a proposed 80/20 model; Stripe Connect payouts are not live.
- Booking, Venmo, Cash App, Airbnb, and VRBO actions use provider handoffs, not privileged partner APIs.
- This private prototype uses its current host for sign-in; a public product needs Pep Rally accounts and entitlement rules.
- Marketplace supply and reviews should be described as prototype examples unless they came from real external creators.

## Do I need a PRD?

Not for tonight. This brief is enough to keep the demo coherent. After the demo, write a lean PRD for the first sellable release: primary buyer and creator, exact transaction, acceptance criteria, entitlement and refund rules, creator review, payout mechanics, hosting boundary, success metrics, and explicit non-goals.

## Best closing line

“People already build tiny apps to solve problems they understand. Pep Rally gives those apps a shelf, a checkout, a trustworthy finish line, and a way for their makers to earn.”

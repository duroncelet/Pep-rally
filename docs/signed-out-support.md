# Signed-out support handoff

The support page and policies publish support@peprally.fun, as requested in the November brief. Signed-out visitors can supply a reply email and open a prefilled email draft without creating an account. They must send it from their email app. The page does not claim automatic delivery. The existing authenticated private-storage path remains optional and does not notify the operator.

This is an email handoff, not an anonymous server-submitted ticket form. If acceptance requires tickets submitted without an email client, that remains separate work with abuse protection and delivery infrastructure.

## Owner verification before launch

- Confirm the mailbox exists and is monitored; this change does not create or verify it.
- From a signed-out browser, open Policies → Support, enter a reply email and message, and open the draft.
- Send a real message from an external email account and confirm receipt and a reply. No delivery test has been performed by this change.
- Check the no-email-client fallback: the address and instructions remain readable and copyable.
- Do not enable the commerce support-ready flag until mailbox receipt and response ownership are verified.

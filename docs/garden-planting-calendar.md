# Planting calendar release

Added alongside the matrix, forecast, care board and journal. Profile and crop timing fields are stored in the existing version-3 garden JSON and included in both Markdown exports; older saves default to empty fields.

The zone and frost dates are explicitly gardener-confirmed, not automatic geospatial lookups. Changing the garden location clears that climate profile. USDA hardiness measures winter cold rather than annual vegetable sowing dates: https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/pages/how-to-use-the-maps

Local lookup links: https://www.almanac.com/gardening/planting-calendar and https://www.almanac.com/gardening/frostdates

Six crop guides cite University of Minnesota Extension individually. Regional calendar dates are not copied into a universal calendar. Tomato indoor sowing is 35–42 days before the gardener's outdoor target; peppers are 56 days before. These are relative estimates, not a guarantee of frost safety. Other crops show condition checks and source links rather than made-up dates. The planting guide explains why soil conditions matter: https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/planting-the-vegetable-garden

Harvest estimates require the gardener's packet days and correct sowing/transplant starting date. The one-week follow-up is an organizer reminder, not a crop-specific watering prescription. Duplicate planting notes and identical care tasks are suppressed. Custom crops, herbs and flowers have no invented variety-level guidance.

Remaining scope: automatic authoritative climate lookup, local-extension discovery, Southern Hemisphere/tropical seasonal models, cultivar catalog, and account-save end-to-end browser verification. No paid almanac content is copied or scraped.
